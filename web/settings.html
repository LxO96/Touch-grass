What you can't turn off

Dangerous heat (feels like 38°C or more), dangerous cold (−15°C or less) and lightning always override the dials. In those conditions the app points you at the safest hour instead of the nearest one — no slider will talk it into sending you out into a thunderstorm.

Otherwise: if you haven't been out yet today, it will never tell you to stay in. The worst it will say is “not yet, go at four”.
