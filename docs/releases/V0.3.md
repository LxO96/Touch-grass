# Touch Grass V0.3

*Your weather, your way, and a reason to look up at night.*

**Download:** `touch-grass-0.3.apk`. Android 7.0 or newer. Installs straight
over V0.2 and keeps your year.

---

## Rate the sky yourself

A clear blue sky and a grey lid used to be scored by our taste. Now you drag
each kind of weather into a band, from **Love it** to **Hate it**, and every
score follows. Thunder stays a fixed penalty, for safety.

## Dusk and dawn

The golden hour was scored like 2am. Twilight now has its own dial, and it
can count as a bonus.

## Firsts

The first rain after two dry weeks, the first snow of the season, the first
sun after a grey stretch and the first warm day each earn a bonus. Some
weather is worth going out for just because it's new.

## The aurora

Touch Grass now reads NOAA's Kp forecast and live aurora map. An hour gets a
bonus when the northern lights are likely where you are, but only if you
could actually see them:

- dark, not twilight
- more than half the sky clear
- no bright moon up

Turn on the aurora alert and it tells you once a night, even late.

## The year, redesigned

- The tabs stay pinned and the title steps aside as you scroll.
- The year is a compact grid, months across and days down.
- Swipe sideways to change month.
- Press and hold a day to set its trips, up to five.

## Your data, safer

- **Automatic backup.** The phone's own backup now carries your year, so a new
  phone or a reinstall brings it back without you doing anything.
- **SAVE** writes a copy wherever you choose. **SHARE** still works.
- **RESTORE FROM FILE**, and restores merge day by day, so an old copy can
  never erase newer days.

## Also

- The daily reminder has its own alarm, so it comes on time and not just as
  you open the app.
- Opening the app now counts as having been reminded.
- The widget shows your recent weeks at three rows high. It used to need four.
- The town search opens on demand and stays above the keyboard.
- The weather credits have moved to Settings.
