# Touch Grass V0.2

*Three forecasts instead of one, and a promise it can actually keep.*

**Download:** `touch-grass-0.2.apk` — Android 7.0 or newer. You'll need to allow
installing from an unknown source.

---

## ⚠️ Read this before you install

**V0.2 cannot install over V0.1.** V0.1 was signed with a throwaway debug key
that no longer exists, so Android will refuse the upgrade. You have to
uninstall first — **and uninstalling wipes your year, your streaks and your
settings.**

Before you uninstall: open **Settings → Your data → SHARE / EMAIL IT** (or
**COPY**) and send the text to yourself. Any copy will restore it once you've
reinstalled.

This is the last time. V0.2 is signed with a proper release key, so every
version after this one will update in place.

## Three weather services, blended

It used to ask Open-Meteo. In the Nordics there are two national services with
better local models, so now it asks all three and weighs them:

| Source | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| MET Norway (YR) | 50% |
| SMHI | 25% |
| Open-Meteo | 25% |

The weights are renormalised hour by hour over whichever sources actually cover
that hour. Outside SMHI's area the blend quietly becomes two sources at 67% and
33%, and the *Where this comes from* card names exactly who answered rather
than claiming a source that didn't.

The blend runs identically in the app and in the home-screen widget, from a
single shared file, so the two can't disagree about the same afternoon.

## Why is this hour rated that?

Tap any bar in the 12-hour chart and it shows its arithmetic:

```
07 — 73/100

Started at        100
Rain  39% chance  −21
Cold  14°C         −6
──────────────────────
                   73

Clears your bar of 60.
```

The score *is* that sum now, rather than a second calculation sitting next to
it, so the bar and the panel can't drift apart. Rows that safety decides —
lightning — are marked as untunable, because no dial should be able to soften
those.

## It only promises you today

The one you'll notice most. An evening forecast reaches into tomorrow morning,
and the app used to happily say *"7am looks properly good — 9 hours off"* and
then, in the same breath, *"that's your one for today."* Seven in the morning
is not today.

Now, while the day is still owed, it only names hours you can still act on.
Once you've been out, tomorrow is fair game again — that's what **STAY IN. YOU
EARNED IT.** was always for. Dangerous weather is the exception and still says
"not tonight" rather than sending you into a thunderstorm.

## Finding your position actually works

It asked only for a cheap network fix with an eight-second timeout, so on any
device with nothing cached it gave up **without ever waking the GPS** — and
then told you the location was probably blocked.

It now asks twice, escalating to the GPS only when it needs to, and never
re-asks after a genuine refusal. The failure message tells the truth about
which happened, and the town-search fallback it points you at now works at all
— it used to throw on every single use.
