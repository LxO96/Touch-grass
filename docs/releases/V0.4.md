# Touch Grass V0.4

*Rain you can see coming, the rest of your day on the home screen, and a
fanfare for going out.*

**Download:** `touch-grass-0.4.apk`. Android 7.0 or newer. Installs straight
over V0.3 and keeps your year.

---

## Rain radar

Turn on **Rain radar on Today** in Settings and Today gets a card for the next
hour's rain:

- **One line that says it plainly**: dry for the next 85 minutes, or rain in
  about 20, light, for half an hour.
- **Five-minute bars** for the next ninety minutes.
- **The last hour of radar** around you, animated, with a dot where you are.
  Zoom out twice to see weather coming from further away.

It uses SMHI's radar in Sweden and MET's in Norway. Elsewhere the line comes
from the forecast model and the map stays hidden. It's off by default, since
the map costs a few hundred kilobytes each time.

## The widget, hour by hour

The taller widget now shows the rest of today hour by hour, with the best hour
picked out, instead of your recent weeks. A wide one-row widget uses its space
for more readings, not empty space.

## A fanfare for going out

Logging your first trip of the day buzzes a little fanfare. Later trips get a
shorter "ba-da-DUM", and taking a trip back buzzes down. The widget's button
buzzes too. Its buzz is a notification vibration, so it follows your phone's
notification vibration setting.

## Also

- **Today** sits right under the verdict, so logging a trip is one tap away.
- Tap an hour in the chart to see its forecast (sky, temperature, feels-like,
  wind and rain) above the score breakdown.
- Fixed: a trip logged on the widget late at night, before the app had picked it
  up, was lost when you logged another the next day. Each one now lands on the
  day you made it.
